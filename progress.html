PROJECT DEVELOPMENT

Progress & Schedule

PACE is being developed through a series of planning, development, integration, and testing phases. This page tracks the team's progress and major project milestones.

DEVELOPMENT PLAN

Project Timeline

PHASE 01

Research & Planning

Define project requirements, research the Formula SAE vehicle systems, and plan the overall hardware and software architecture.

PHASE 02

System Development

Develop the software components needed to collect, process, and display vehicle data.

PHASE 03

Hardware Integration

Integrate the software with the Raspberry Pi, vehicle communication systems, and other required hardware.

PHASE 04

Testing & Refinement

Test the complete system, evaluate performance, resolve issues, and improve reliability.

PHASE 05

Final Delivery

Complete final documentation, demonstrations, presentations, and delivery of the finished PACE system.
