Formula SAE

What is FSAE

Formula SAE is a student design competition organized by the Society of Automotive Engineers (SAE). The competition challenges students to design, build, and compete with small formula-style race cars. Teams are judged on various aspects of their vehicles, including design, performance, and cost-effectiveness.

2027 FSAE CAR

Our Car

Our team is currently working on the 2027 FSAE car, which is designed to compete in the Formula SAE competition. The car features a lightweight chassis, advanced aerodynamics, and a high-performance engine. Our team is focused on optimizing the car's performance through innovative engineering solutions and cutting-edge technology.

OUR PROJECT

PACE

Our team PACE will help the engineers in Formula SAE who want to actively monitor vehicle systems to help optimize vehicle performance for competition by developing interface software to handle data transmission for the vehicle.
